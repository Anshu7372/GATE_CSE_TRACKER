### Recognize vs decide
- **M recognizes L**: w ∈ L → M accept karta (halt). w ∉ L → reject kare **ya loop kare** (kabhi halt na kare). Ye recursively enumerable (RE) languages ke liye.
- **M decides L**: w ∈ L → accept; w ∉ L → **reject (halt)**. M **hamesha halt** karta. Ye recursive (decidable) languages ke liye.

### Options analysis
- (A) sirf "sab pe halt" — par kya accept/reject sahi hai, ye nahi kehta.
- (B) sirf L ke strings accept — baaki pe loop kar sakta (ye recognize hai).
- (C) sirf complement reject — L ke strings ke baare me kuch nahi.
- (D) L accept **aur** complement reject → har input pe halt + sahi answer ✓.
== tips
- **Decider = total TM** (always halts). Ek line: "accept L, reject L̄".
== sol
Decide = accept every w ∈ L and reject every w ∉ L (dono cases me halt). Ye exactly (D) hai; A, B, C me se har ek adhoora hai.

**Answer: (D)**
